01 · Build

The website

Your clients look you up on Google before they call. If they don't find you, or they find a site from 2015, they call the competitor instead. A custom, bilingual, fast site puts you in the running — a generic template doesn't.

  • Custom design, approved as a mockup
  • French and English, on every page
  • Desktop, tablet and phone
  • Local SEO for Ottawa–Gatineau
  • Google Business Profile connected to the site
  • Launched on your own domain

Not: a purchased theme filled in with your copy.

02 · Image

The images

Stock photography is recognisable, and it tells a client you have nothing to show. Real images of your space and your team do the opposite. They work on the site, on your Google profile and on social.

  • Professional photo session on site
  • One video reel, ready for social
  • Team portraits and workspace shots
  • Professional retouching included
  • Images are yours, no usage limits

Not: rented photos you lose at the end of the contract.

03 · Trajectory

The plan

A website doesn't fix anything on its own. If your sales stall somewhere else — on the phone, in follow-up, in what you charge — the site will only make the problem more visible. We look at the whole picture before building.

  • Audit of marketing, sales and delivery
  • A clear objective, in numbers
  • Detailed strategy, step by step
  • Four sessions with a consultant
  • Six years in ad management

Not: a generic report nobody reads twice.
