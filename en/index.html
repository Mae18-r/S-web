04 — The process

How it works.

Five steps, in this order. At every point you know where the project stands and what's expected from you.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We look at where you are now, what you sell, to whom, and what's blocking you online. No preparation needed.

    On your side: thirty minutes.

  2. 02

    Detailed proposal

    You get everything in writing before you commit: what's included, what costs more, response times and terms.

    On your side: read it, ask your questions.

  3. 03

    Audit, objective and mockup

    The consultant reviews your marketing, sales and service delivery. We define where you want to get to, and you see the full site before a single line is coded.

    On your side: your current numbers, your copy, your logo.

  4. 04

    Photo session and build

    The photographer comes to you while we build the site. Both move in parallel.

    On your side: half a day on site, your team available.

  5. 05

    Launch and follow-up

    The site goes live on your domain. Then: SEO checks, monthly report, adjustments.

    On your side: nothing. That part is ours.
